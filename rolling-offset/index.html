What the words mean

TermMeaning
RiseHow far the pipe moves vertically.
RollHow far the pipe moves sideways. Rise and roll are at right angles to each other.
True offsetThe straight-line distance between the two pipe centerlines, square root of rise squared plus roll squared. This is the number you use as the "set" of an ordinary offset.
TravelCenter-to-center length of the diagonal pipe between the two fittings. True offset divided by sin of the fitting angle.
Set-backHow far along the main line the offset takes. True offset divided by tan of the fitting angle.
Roll angleThe tilt of the offset plane, measured from the rise direction, atan of roll over rise. Rise 6 and roll 8 gives 53.13 degrees.

How to use the result

Subtract the take-out of each fitting from the travel to get the cut length of the diagonal pipe. Take-out depends on the fitting type, pipe size and weld gap, so look it up for your fitting. Set the two fittings so the plane through both pipes is tilted by the roll angle from vertical, then check the first one with a level before you tack.

Fitting factors for a simple offset, travel and set-back per 1 unit of offset: 60 degrees 1.1547 and 0.5774, 45 degrees 1.4142 and 1.0, 30 degrees 2.0 and 1.7321, 22.5 degrees 2.6131 and 2.4142, 11.25 degrees 5.1258 and 5.0273.

Worked check: rise 16, roll 12, 45 degree fittings. True offset 20, travel 28.28. The 12-16-20 triangle is the 3-4-5 triangle scaled up, so you can verify it by hand.

Questions

Is a rolling offset the same as a simple offset?

A simple offset moves the pipe in one plane. A rolling offset moves it in two directions at once, up and sideways. Once you reduce rise and roll to one true offset, the rest of the math is the same as a simple offset in a tilted plane.

Does the angle have to be 45 degrees?

No. Any fitting angle works if both fittings match. 45 is the most common because the travel is 1.4142 times the offset. Pick Custom for anything else.

Does this subtract fitting take-out?

No. Results are center-to-center, so you can use them with any fitting type. Subtract the take-out for your fitting and weld gap yourself.

Does anything get sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded or stored.
