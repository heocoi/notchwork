Privacy Policy for Cut List

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Cut List is published on Google Play by DEMETIO, JSC. ("we").

What we collect. Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not send anything you enter to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device. Your parts, sheet sizes, projects and settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them. A PDF you export goes wherever you choose to share or save it.

Purchases. The optional one-time Pro upgrade is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We receive no card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro was purchased on your account.

Permissions. The app requests no runtime permissions.

The web version. The free web planner on this site runs in your browser. Your list is kept only in that browser's local storage and is never uploaded.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes. If this changes, we will update this page and the date above before shipping the new version.
