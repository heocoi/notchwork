How the layout is made

The layout uses a guillotine method: every cut runs edge to edge, as on a table saw or panel saw. The page runs the same packer under many sort orders, fit rules and split rules and keeps the result that leaves the least unplaced area, then the fewest sheet area, sheets and cuts. Check the plan before you cut expensive material.
