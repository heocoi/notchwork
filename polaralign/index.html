Polar Align for Android

See where Polaris goes in your polar scope right now, for your site and the current time. Put the star on the dot, lock the mount, start imaging.

Coming soon to Google Play

What it shows

  • The polar scope reticle with Polaris, or sigma Octantis in the south, at the right clock position
  • The dial reading in large type, plus the hour angle and the time to the next upper transit
  • Through-the-scope view (turned 180°) or naked-eye view

Made for the field

It works offline. Type your coordinates, or tap "Use my location once"; the position never leaves your phone. Red night mode hides the system bars so nothing white stays on screen. No ads, no account.

Free and Pro

Free: the clock-face reticle with the live star position, in both hemispheres. Pro is a one-time purchase and adds Sky-Watcher, iOptron, Orion and Celestron plates drawn from the manufacturers' manuals, a 24-hour dial with Polaris year rings in the north, dial rotation and mirroring, a time planner and saved sites.

Accuracy

Positions include precession, nutation and aberration. We compared the hour angle with the astropy library on 260 dates from 2026 to 2035 in both hemispheres; the largest difference was under 0.1 arcminute. Atmospheric refraction is not applied.
