Privacy Policy for Pipe Offset Calculator

Last updated: 2026-11-01

Pipe Offset Calculator is published on Google Play by DEMETIO, JSC. ("we").

What we collect. Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not send anything you enter to us or to anyone else. It has no internet permission.

What stays on your device. The measurements you type and your settings are kept only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Purchases. The optional one-time Pro upgrade is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We receive no card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro was purchased on your account.

Permissions. The app requests no runtime permissions.

The web version. The free rolling offset calculator on this site runs in your browser and stores nothing.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes. If this changes, we will update this page and the date above before shipping the new version.
