The first-step mistake

Drop the first cut by the tread thickness. Every tread sits on top of the stringer, so a 1" tread adds 1" to the first step. If you cut the bottom of the stringer with a full riser, the first step comes out one tread thickness too tall, and every other step is correct. Code limits riser variation in a flight to 3/8", so a 1" error at the bottom fails inspection, and it is the step people trip on.

The fix is to cut the bottom plumb cut at riser minus tread thickness. This calculator does that for you and shows it in the drawing as the first cut. The top works the same way: the last seat is placed so the floor above lands at the full rise. If your top floor finish is a different thickness than your treads, adjust the top by the difference.

This page does not check stringer spacing or span, handrails and guards, winders, spiral stairs, or stairs outside the IRC. Treat the output as a layout aid, and test-fit a stringer before you cut all of them.

How the numbers are worked out

  • Risers = total rise divided by 7 3/4", rounded up. Riser height = total rise divided by risers.
  • Treads = risers minus 1, because the floor above is the last tread. With a landing, each flight has its own risers minus 1 treads.
  • First cut = riser minus tread thickness. Each seat is marked at step number times riser, minus tread thickness, so rounding never builds up along the stringer.
  • Pitch length is the distance from notch tip to notch tip. Board length is the long edge of the stock, long point to long point.

Questions

Do I measure rise to the finished floor?

Yes, finished floor to finished floor, from the surface where the bottom step starts to the surface of the floor above. If the finish is not installed yet, add its thickness to the rise you measured.

Why is the stringer shorter or longer than other calculators say?

Many tools report the diagonal of total rise and total run. The notch layout above has one fewer tread than risers and a first cut shortened by the tread thickness, so the board length here comes from the actual shape and the stock width you picked.

What if the run does not fit?

Enter your run available and leave the tread blank. The tread is sized to fit, rounded down to 1/16". If it falls below 10" you will see a failure, and the options are a landing, a different layout, or more room.

Does anything get sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded or stored.
