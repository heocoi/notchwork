Polar Align

Where Polaris goes in your polar scope, right now.

Shows where Polaris, or sigma Octantis in the south, goes in your polar scope for your site and the current time. Put the star on the dot, lock the mount, start imaging.

Works offline. No ads, no account. Red night mode for the field.
